COLOURS (tuned for screens from a printed swatch book)

  png/    transparent PNG, 1600-3000 px wide (on-red files include their red background)
  poses/  the four eye poses of the symbol (looking, left, down, found), SVG and PNG
  icons/  favicon.svg, favicon-32.png, apple-touch-icon-180.png, app-icon-512.png, avatar-round-800.png

NAMING   adsdetective_[design]_[colourway]
  designs:    symbol, wordmark, wordmark-stacked, lockup-horizontal, lockup-vertical
  colourways: red (on light), blue (on light), cameo (on red or dark), on-red (sticker with light outline and red field)

SMALLEST SIZES   symbol 24 px / 10 mm, horizontal lockup 160 px / 35 mm

DO NOT   stretch, rotate, add a mouth, shadow or outline, recolour outside the palette, or redraw the detective.
