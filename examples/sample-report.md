# Ad research: mobile card game (United Kingdom, November 2026)

> Example written by hand with invented advertisers. It shows the format. It is not output from a real search.

## 1. Brief

- Project: a mobile card battler. Goal: installs. Audience: players who like deck builders.
- Niche: mobile games. Country: United Kingdom. Platforms: Meta, TikTok.
- Competitors covered: Sample Card Battler, Sample Studio Alpha, Sample Cloud Play (all invented).
- Defaults used: find the main competitors, goal = installs.

## 2. Top ads

| Advertiser | Platform | Days running | Versions | Format | Hook | Angle | Offer | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sample Studio Alpha | Meta | 214 | 9 | Video | A player wins with a bad hand | Skill beats luck | Free first week | Medium |
| Sample Cloud Play | Meta, TikTok | 58 | 7 | Video | A phone on a train | Play anywhere | Free trial | Medium |
| Sample Card Battler | Meta | 12 | 5 | Image | Two decks side by side | Build and compare | Install | Low |

Visual notes for the first ad: phone-shot clip, face and screen in frame, large caption in the first second, warm colours, 9:16.

## 3. Patterns

- Skill beats luck: 2 of 3 advertisers.
- Play anywhere: 1 of 3.

## 4. Visual patterns

- Phone-shot video with a caption in the first second: 2 of 3.
- Polished game footage: 1 of 3.

## 5. Landing page notes

The longest-running advertiser sends visitors to a page with the offer above the fold and one install button.

## 6. Gaps and opportunities

1. Social play, such as challenging a friend. No advertiser leads with it.
2. Short tutorials as the hook. Everyone starts with a win.
3. Static comparison images. Almost all ads are video.

## 7. Test plan

1. **Friend challenge.** Hook: "Beat my deck." Angle: social. Offer: install. Brief: two phone screens, handheld, caption in the first second.
2. **Ten second lesson.** Hook: "Learn the game in ten seconds." Angle: easy start. Offer: free first week. Brief: screen recording with a voice-over.
3. **Deck comparison.** Hook: "Which deck wins?" Angle: curiosity. Offer: install. Brief: static image, two decks, a question.

## 8. Limits

Invented data. Real libraries show no spend or conversions. Run time is a signal, not proof.
